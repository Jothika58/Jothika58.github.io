// About
Who I Am

I am currently pursuing my M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at SASTRA Deemed University, with a focus on deep learning, computer vision, and natural language processing. My research interests lie in building classification systems for medical imaging and cybersecurity applications, and I have had the opportunity to publish my work through Scopus-indexed journals, IEEE conferences, an IGI Global book chapter, and an Indian patent.

My recent projects include a brain tumor classification system using InceptionResNetV2 (98.25% accuracy on 7,023 MRI images) and a calibrated multi-feature SVM framework for spam detection (99.09% on UCI SMS Spam). I enjoy working on end-to-end pipelines — from data preprocessing and feature engineering through model evaluation and interpretation.

Outside of research, I have been involved in student communities as IEEE Web Master, Google DSC Data Science Head, and GFG Vice-Chairperson, and have conducted workshops on machine learning and deep learning. I am looking for opportunities in AI/ML engineering or applied data science where I can continue learning while contributing to meaningful, real-world projects.

Quality-aware pseudo labelling framework using frozen DINOv2 features for label-efficient medical image classification. Employs feature norm, k-NN density, and prediction entropy as quality signals.
